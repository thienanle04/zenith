# Physical holdings have no stock account

Gold in hand is a holding on the portfolio. It has a quantity and is valued with the instrument's price. It has no broker cash behind it. Buying more is a trade against the cash or bank account that paid. Shares stay different: each share holding belongs to a stock account, and the trade uses that account's cash.

Giving drawer gold a stock account would invent a cash balance the owner does not have at a broker.
