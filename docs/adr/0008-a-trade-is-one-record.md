# A trade is one record, fee included

A buy or a sell stores quantity, cash, and fee together. The fee counts as an expense under the built-in fee category. Deleting the trade deletes the cash change, the quantity change, and the fee together. The cash stored on the trade is what was paid or received. Net worth values the holding at quantity times the current price, so selling does not also post the profit as income.

A separate expense for the fee could be deleted while the shares remained. Posting profit as income on top of a mark-to-market price would count that profit twice.
