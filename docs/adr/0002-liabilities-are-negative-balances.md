# Liabilities are negative balances

A credit card or loan is money the owner owes. Zenith stores that balance as a negative number in the same way an asset stores a positive one, so net worth is the sum of every account balance plus holdings.

The alternative was a positive "amount owed" plus a liability flag that every total would have to subtract. One signed balance keeps a balance adjustment as a single signed difference: moving a card from 0 to −3,000,000 is the whole entry.
