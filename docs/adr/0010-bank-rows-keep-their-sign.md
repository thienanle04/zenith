# A bank row is stored with the sign the bank shows

A photo prefills the expense form, and the owner saves it as a transaction. A bank row posts immediately on the linked account, and the same bank reference does not post twice. The amount stored is the signed amount in the feed. A credit-card purchase that the bank shows as −200,000 is stored as −200,000, an expense. The adapter does not flip liability rows.

Flipping card charges would turn a feed that already matches the ledger into income.
