# Net worth is the current figure

v1 shows net worth as of now. An instrument has one current price, and the rate list has one current rate per currency. Neither keeps history, so the app does not chart net worth over time. Income and expense totals still cover a period, because each transaction keeps its own time, category, and exchange rate. A past account balance is the sum of its transactions up to that time.

Keeping a price series would make today's net worth depend on a history the owner did not ask to maintain.
