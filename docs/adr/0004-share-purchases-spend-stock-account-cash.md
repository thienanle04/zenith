# Share purchases spend cash already in a stock account

The owner can keep more than one stock account. Buying shares spends that account's cash, so the money is transferred in from a bank, cash, or e-wallet first. The purchase itself is not a transfer and is not an expense, except for the fee.

A holding belongs to one stock account. The same instrument in two stock accounts is two holdings, and one account's cash cannot pay for the other's trade. A buy that would make the stock account's cash negative is refused. Sale proceeds come back to that account.

Paying the bank directly on a buy would hide the cash sitting at the broker and would mix a funding transfer with the trade.
